# Narrative Spine — the one story everything else selects from

Written 2026-09-09. This is the master narrative for the TNC application. Letter, essay, and form answers are *selections* from this, never contradictions of it.

## The thesis of the life

> A life spent being pushed out of spaces and learning how spaces are drawn — and therefore how they can be redrawn.

Every episode below is an instance of the same structure: **a space (of study, of work, of sound, of knowledge, of public reality) whose boundaries were decided by someone else, and the slow discovery that the real power lies not in moving inside the space but in the architecture that defines it.** That is exactly what the Information Architecture & Intelligence Design program calls "how hierarchies of intelligence come into being, and which forms are protected, cultivated, or sidelined."

Creativity, for Kaveh, is the name of the capacity to redraw a space. Cybernetics is the language that let him see spaces as systems with purposes and feedback. Kavian is the attempt to give that capacity back to a people whose space of knowing has been seized. Healthcare is where he sees the capacity used *for* people. AI is the field where the question is now unavoidable.

## The episodes (in order)

### 1. Rank 301 — the space taken away
- National university entrance exam: **rank 301** nationally (among hundreds of thousands). Admitted to the **University of Tehran**, computer engineering — the top of the space.
- **Editorial decision (2026-09-09):** the rank appears ONCE, in the essay §1, framed with explicit humility ("not out of pride… because it matters to the argument"). It is deliberately kept OUT of the Letter of Intent, where it would read as a credential to a school that critiques hierarchies of intelligence.
- Dismissed for **cultural and political activity** as a student.
- Lesson: a space can be taken from you by someone who controls its boundary; merit inside the space does not protect you from the architecture around it.

### 2. Starting from zero — and the dark years
- Began again at **Azad University**, civil engineering: from the top of one space to the bottom of another.
- Frustration, dark times, mental struggle. **Survived.** (Say it plainly; no melodrama.)
- Lesson: the second ascent is a different kind of knowledge than the first — you learn what a constraint actually is.

### 3. Seventy-two strings — constraint as the condition of beauty
- **Santur**, 20+ years. Tuning, the *radif*, the strike.
- The instrument as proof that everything beautiful happens inside constraints, not despite them.
- During the dark years the santur was the one space that could not be taken.
- Works with galleries, artists, architects — ideation and implementation.

### 4. Builder — infrastructure to AI
- 2004: control software for **high-voltage substations** (ITA).
- A decade+ leading **construction, steel, infrastructure** (lean re-engineering 400→1,000 t/month).
- Product, marketplace, ERP across 14 subsidiaries, healthcare product.
- 2021–2025: **production AI** — five systems in daily use; forecasting (~12% fewer stock-outs); risk detection lag 10+ days → <24h; RAG/agents; human review gates.
- Discovery: the systems moved the **location of judgment** upstream and made it invisible. Information architecture = where the decision about what counts gets made, and by whom.

### 5. The obsession — creativity, technically, philosophically, existentially
- Watching ChatGPT from the first day. Following AI artists (Anadol). Conviction: most "AI art" **samples** a space; it does not **transform** it.
- Boden's distinction (exploratory vs **transformational** creativity) gives the technical form; Negarestani's "mind as artefact of its own conception" the philosophical; the santur strike the existential.
- Why it is urgent now: we are building systems that will decide what the space of the possible looks like for everyone. If they can only sample, we will all be living inside the average.

### 6. Cybernetics — the language he loves
- **Wiener**: feedback, purpose, *The Human Use of Human Beings*; "to live effectively is to live with adequate information"; the danger of surrendering judgment to fixed-objective machines.
- **Ashby**: Law of Requisite Variety — only variety can absorb variety; a regulator must match the variety of what it regulates. (Applies to art, to oversight of AI, to a dictatorship reducing citizens' variety.)
- **Stafford Beer**: the purpose of a system is what it does (POSIWID) — the tool for judging AI, art, and states by behaviour not intention.
- **Bateson**: information is a difference that makes a difference — creativity as the difference the system did not contain.

### 7. Healthcare — AI *for* humans
- JDCS 2026, second author: federated vs centralized LLMs at surgical edge; centralized 43% faster, FL ~5% energy overhead; Jetson Orin Nano (HIL simulation — do NOT claim physical boards); "price of privacy."
- Lesson: the **architecture is the ethics** — expressed in latency and joules. Healthcare is where the constraint (a body, a ten-millisecond window) makes AI honest.

### 8. Forty-eight hours in January — Kavian from the heart
- In the streets during the **Mahsa Amini** uprising (2022).
- **In Iran during 8–9 January 2026**, when security forces killed thousands within roughly forty-eight hours under a near-total **internet blackout**.
- A blackout is information architecture used as a weapon: the state seized the space of knowing so that the killing could not be counted.
- Lesson: Ashby in the street — a regime survives by reducing the variety of what its citizens can know.
- **Kavian**: an evidence-first, anti-capture public platform — every claim typed (fact / estimate / analysis / proposal), sourced, with confidence; right of reply archived; auditable reasoning; human refusal gate; founder sunset clause. Not to take power; to give people back the power to know.

### 9. The New Centre — where the questions become one field
- IA&ID interrogates intelligence as technique **and** as screen for power; Intelligence Design Lab builds with agentic tools while questioning them.
- Seminars: Billuart (flesh to interface), Lê (new rationalisms/AI), Longo (informational war for subjectivity), Schirren (shape of information), Grunthaner (intelligibility and technics), Pedroso (ghostweb), Rastegar (Satrapi — reframing Iran).
- What he brings: two decades of consequential systems + the practitioner's habit of testing ideas against what breaks + a life that has felt what information architecture does.
